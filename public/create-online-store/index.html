Create an online shop in Kenya with your own branded storefront.

DASMARKET gives Kenyan businesses a practical way to establish a dedicated online shop without building an ecommerce system from the ground up. Set up your seller account, organise your products and publish a storefront customers can visit from phones or computers.

A shop built around your business

Your storefront is the public home of your business online. Present your own products, prices, images and shop information in one place. Customers can browse your catalogue, add items to a cart and place orders through your shop.

02 / DASMARKET

Keep daily operations together

Use the seller workspace to update products and stock, review incoming orders, follow fulfilment and manage shop settings. Run your website and order process from one account.

  • Add and organise products with images and prices
  • Review orders and delivery information
  • Set customer-facing shop policies
  • Connect a domain you already own when ready
03 / DASMARKET

Choose how your customers pay

Configure the payment and payout options available to your shop. Where supported, you can set your own merchant Till or Paybill details so payments are directed according to your configured merchant setup. Check each transaction status before fulfilling an order.

Questions about this part of your online shop?

How do I start an online store?

Create a seller account, choose your shop name, add your products and complete the shop setup in your dashboard. You can then publish and share your shop address.

Do I need to build a website myself?

DASMARKET provides the storefront and ecommerce workflow, so you do not need to develop a shop system from scratch.

Can I use my own domain name?

Yes. Start with your DASMARKET shop address and connect a domain you own through the seller dashboard.
