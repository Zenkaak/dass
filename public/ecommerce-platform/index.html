ONE PLATFORM FOR YOUR SHOP

An ecommerce platform for the way your business sells.

DASMARKET brings the customer-facing shop and the seller tools behind it into one connected platform. It is designed for retailers, wholesalers, independent brands and growing businesses that want to sell through their own online storefront.

03 / DASMARKET

Your business remains your business

Each seller operates a branded shop with its own products, customer-facing information and configured payment destination. DASMARKET supplies the ecommerce infrastructure; you remain responsible for your products, service, fulfilment and shop policies.

Questions about this part of your online shop?

Who can use DASMARKET?

Retailers, wholesalers, independent brands and other businesses that want to operate a dedicated online shop can create a seller account.

Does DASMARKET include order management?

Yes. Sellers can review and manage orders from the seller workspace, alongside products, stock and shop settings.

Can each business have its own branded shop?

Yes. Each seller storefront can present the seller’s own shop identity, catalogue and customer-facing policies.
