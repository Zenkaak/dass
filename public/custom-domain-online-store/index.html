Connect your own domain to your online shop.

A domain that matches your business name makes your shop easier to recognise and share. With DASMARKET, begin with the shop address provided during setup and connect a domain you own when you are ready.

01 / DASMARKET

Start with a shop address

When you create a shop, DASMARKET provides a shareable shop address on the DASMARKET domain. Use it while preparing your catalogue and introducing your online store to customers.

02 / DASMARKET

Move to a domain you own

If you already own a domain, use the domain connection process in your seller dashboard. Follow the DNS instructions shown for your shop and allow time for the configuration to take effect. Keep your original DASMARKET shop address available as a fallback.

03 / DASMARKET

Keep the storefront connected

Your custom domain points customers to your existing shop. Product management, order handling and shop configuration remain available through your DASMARKET seller account.

  • Use a domain that reflects your business name
  • Follow the DNS records provided in your dashboard
  • Check the domain connection status before sharing widely

Questions about this part of your online shop?

Do I need to buy a domain before creating a shop?

No. You can start with the DASMARKET shop address and connect a domain you own later.

Where do I connect my domain?

Use the domain settings in your seller dashboard and follow the DNS instructions displayed for your shop.

Will I lose my shop if I connect a custom domain?

Your shop remains managed from the same seller account. The original DASMARKET address remains available.
