PAYMENTS FOR YOUR ONLINE SHOP

Sell online in Kenya with supported M-PESA merchant payments.

A useful online shop needs more than a product page. Customers need a clear checkout, and sellers need to know whether a payment has been confirmed. DASMARKET connects your storefront, order workflow and configured payment options in one place.

01 / DASMARKET

Configure your merchant payment details

Set up the payment destination for your shop in seller settings. Supported M-PESA merchant configurations can use the relevant Till or Paybill details. The payment route depends on the merchant details and options configured for your shop.

02 / DASMARKET

Follow payment and order status

Review the transaction status associated with an order before preparing or dispatching it. Keeping payment confirmation and order fulfilment as separate checks helps reduce mistakes.

  • Configure supported merchant payment details
  • Review payment status against the order
  • Keep order references for follow-up
  • Use order reporting if a payment or order needs attention
03 / DASMARKET

Keep the checkout connected to your shop

Customers order from your branded storefront. The seller workspace gives you the order information needed to process the sale and coordinate delivery according to your shop policies.

Questions about this part of your online shop?

Can I connect my own M-PESA Till or Paybill?

DASMARKET supports seller payment configuration for supported merchant Till and Paybill setups. Configure the merchant details available to your shop in seller settings.

Do payments automatically mean an order is ready to dispatch?

No. Check the confirmed payment status and the order details before fulfilment.

Can I manage orders and payments in one place?

The seller workspace brings order management and payment status visibility together with your other shop controls.
