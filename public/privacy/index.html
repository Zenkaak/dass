Privacy Policy

How DASMARKET handles account, shop, order and support information.

PRIVACY NOTICE · LAST UPDATED 12 NOVEMBER 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DASMARKET handles personal information when you visit our website, create an account, operate a shop or use platform features. It should be read alongside the privacy information published by individual sellers and any payment or delivery providers you choose to use.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

DASMARKET is responsible for personal information it handles to operate its website, user accounts, platform security and support. A seller who operates a shop is separately responsible for personal information collected through that shop for the seller's own business purposes, such as fulfilling orders and responding to customers. Depending on the feature and arrangement, DASMARKET may also process information on a seller's behalf to provide platform services.

2. Information we may collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, login and account settings.
  • Shop information: shop name, business details, branding, product listings and configuration.
  • Order information: products ordered, order references, buyer and seller details, delivery address and order status.
  • Payment information: payment method, transaction reference, amount and payment status supplied by the relevant provider or merchant integration. Do not send us your M-PESA PIN, password or full card security details.
  • Support and reports: messages, complaints, attachments and information you provide when asking for help.
  • Technical information: device and browser details, IP address, log events and information needed to protect the platform.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts, publish and operate shops, process and display orders, support configured checkout and payment features, communicate service notices, respond to support requests, investigate reports, prevent fraud and abuse, secure and improve the platform, and meet legal obligations.

4. Legal grounds for processing

Where required by applicable law, we process information because it is necessary to provide the service or perform an agreement with you, to comply with a legal obligation, for legitimate interests such as platform security and fraud prevention, or because you have given consent for a specific purpose. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it, subject to legal limitations.

5. When information is shared

Information is shared only where needed for the purposes described in this policy, including with the seller and buyer involved in an order; payment, hosting, security, communication and other technology providers that support platform operations; professional advisers; and public authorities where disclosure is required or permitted by law. We do not sell personal information to third parties for their own marketing.

6. Seller-managed customer information

When you buy from a seller's shop, that seller receives information needed to process and fulfil your order and manage the customer relationship. The seller is responsible for explaining its own privacy practices and responding to requests about information it controls. Please contact the seller directly for questions about how it uses information for its own business purposes. DASMARKET can assist with platform-related privacy requests.

7. International processing

Some service providers may process information in countries outside Kenya. Where this occurs, we take steps required by applicable data-protection law to provide appropriate safeguards for the transfer and processing of personal information.

8. Retention

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the platform, maintain account and transaction records, resolve reports and disputes, protect users, meet legal obligations and enforce our agreements. Retention periods vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected. Information is deleted, anonymised or securely archived when it is no longer required, subject to lawful retention needs and backup cycles.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Please protect your login details and contact us promptly if you suspect your account has been compromised.

10. Your data-protection rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing, or a copy of information in a portable format. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent and lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya. We may need to verify your identity and may be unable to fulfil a request where a lawful exception applies. For information controlled by a seller, please contact that seller in the first instance.

11. Cookies and similar technologies

DASMARKET may use essential cookies or browser storage for sign-in, session security, preferences and core platform functionality. If analytics or other optional technologies are used, they should be managed in accordance with applicable consent requirements. Blocking essential cookies may affect how the service works.

12. Children's information

DASMARKET is a business and ecommerce platform and is not intended for children to create seller accounts. Sellers must comply with applicable laws when offering products or collecting information relating to children.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the platform, our practices or legal requirements. The latest version and its revision date will be published on this page.

14. Contact and complaints

For privacy questions or requests relating to information handled by DASMARKET, contact support@dasmarket.co.ke or call +254 758 884 861. You may also contact the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya if you believe your data-protection rights have been infringed.
