Privacy Policy

This notice describes the categories of information that may be handled when you use DASMARKET, why that information is needed and how to contact us with a privacy question. It applies to the DASMARKET platform and not to separate privacy practices of individual sellers or external providers.

1. Information handled

Depending on how you use the platform, information may include account and contact details, shop profile information, product and catalogue content, customer orders, delivery details, payment references and transaction status, support messages, reports and technical or security logs.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, operate storefronts, process and display orders, support merchant-configured checkout, provide customer and seller support, investigate reports, prevent misuse, maintain platform security and improve the service.

3. Seller and customer roles

Sellers control the product, order, delivery and customer information they collect through their shops and are responsible for providing customers with appropriate notices and handling requests relating to their own business. DASMARKET processes platform information to provide and protect the service and may handle information as required by applicable law.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information may be shared with relevant sellers and customers to complete shop and order activity, with technology or payment providers where needed to operate features, and with authorities where disclosure is required by law. We do not publish private account credentials or payment secrets.

5. Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including service operation, dispute handling, security and legal obligations.

6. Your choices and requests

You may update available account details through your account settings. For a privacy question or request concerning information handled by DASMARKET, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity and may be required to retain certain records.

7. Cookies and technical data

The platform may use essential browser storage, cookies or similar technologies for sign-in, session security, preferences and service functionality. Disabling these technologies may affect some features.

8. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the platform or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its revision date.

9. Contact
