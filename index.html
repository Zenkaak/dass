Create an online shop in Kenya with DASMARKET

DASMARKET is an ecommerce platform for Kenyan businesses, retailers, wholesalers and entrepreneurs who want to sell through their own branded online store. Create a shop, publish your products, receive customer orders and manage everyday operations from one seller account.

Build an ecommerce website without developing a shopping system from scratch. Your storefront gives customers a place to browse products, view prices and place orders from a phone or computer. You control your shop identity, catalogue and customer-facing information.

Build and manage your online store

Add product names, descriptions, images, prices and stock details. Use your seller dashboard to update your catalogue, review incoming orders, coordinate delivery and keep shop policies current. The platform brings your public storefront and seller workspace together.

Configure M-PESA merchant payments

Set up the supported payment options available to your business. Where configured, sellers can use their own M-PESA Till or Paybill merchant details. Review the confirmed payment status and order information before fulfilling a customer order.

Connect a custom domain

Start with a DASMARKET shop address and connect a domain you own when you are ready. A domain that matches your business name gives customers a familiar web address to visit and share.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create an online shop in Kenya?

Create a seller account, choose your shop name, add products and shop information, configure supported payment options, then publish and share your storefront.

Can I use my own domain for my online store?

Yes. Start with your DASMARKET shop address and connect a domain you own through the seller dashboard.

Can customers pay with M-PESA?

Sellers can configure supported M-PESA merchant Till or Paybill details for their shop. Available payment options depend on the merchant configuration.

Can I manage products and orders from one dashboard?

Yes. The seller workspace includes product, stock, order, payment and shop-management tools.
